#Project: Plant Care Guide

## Miriam Carbajal

### 1. Project Purpose
* Guiding people who want to start planting
* Proving visuals 
* Showing different caring steps for different plants
* Providing water and soil guides
* Sharing fun facts of different plants and the energy they can bring to a space
* Informing people who want to start planting or those who already do of how to care for each plant

### Project Description
An informative guide for all levels of planting. Providing information on each plant and their care. It will also include pictures and tips to growing healthy plants. Information will include  plants and their respective sun exposure, water schedules and potting schedules. I will provide ideal areas in which the plants should be placed for the most effective growth. Website will be simple and easy to navigate. 

### Desired Results
1. Have information that can help those at all levels of plant care
2. Provide clear pictures and links for information necessary 
3. Make the website as smooth as possible for an easy user experience
4. Serve as a reference users can come back to too at any time
