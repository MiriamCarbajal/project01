Caring for your Plants

No matter your level of experience with plant care, this guide will be able to help with any questions on caring for your plants. Plants are good to have in household for many reasons. Some of those reasons include: fresh oxygen, lowers stress, boost happiness, and they make good decor when done appropriately.

Guide to Appropriate Sunlight

  • Direct Light Succulents, Lavender, Aloe Vera, Geraniums, Roses
  • Partial Sun Snake Plant, Jade Plant, Peace Lily, Calathea, Croton
  • Indirect Light Spider Plant, Pothos, Fiddle Leaf Fig, Orchid, Monstera, Bromeliad

Tips to Remember

  • Soil Moisture You should check the moisture of the soil before watering again.
  • Repotting When repotting you must leave 1/3 of the new pot with fresh soild in order to leave space for root growth.
  • Drainage Ensure that the pots have holes at the bottom to allow proper drainage.

Fun Plant Facts

Household plants are known to improve energy and bring peace. They do so by cleaning the air, and lowering stress hormones which in turn bring some calmness into a space. Some plants have a symbol that most people know them for. For example, Lavender is promoted to reduce anxiety and insomnia. Aloe Vera is promoted to bring healing energy and many use it for home remedies. The Jade Plant symbolises goodluck and in some cultures is known as the "money plant".
